Distant Memories

8mm Film transferred to HD. 3 Channel Installation. 2017

My connection to the Philippines are deeply rooted in my parents, they are the bridge to some part of myself & so my process for my artwork was apparent to me when I took the opportunity when my parents visited the Philippines this year in March. I gave them a super 8 camera, with 2 rolls of 8mm film and asked them to film what they remember the most about the Philippines, asking them to rewrite their memories.
