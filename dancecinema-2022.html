Curatorial statement

By Melissa Ramos

‘8 Hrs a Day’ curation focuses on dance films that discerns the effects of capitalisation embodied within labour and the entrapments of structures that bind people to the motions of debt. This series of dance films explore narratives that reveal the many layers of how this disrupts, deceive, disconnect and entangle our body & mind.

‘TrainEscape’ by Francesca Gozdek (Italy) frames the fragility within working individuals, traversing through the symmetry of systems. Designed to comply to the frameworks of labour, here we are taken through the psychological worlds of each dancer. Wearisome, disfigured, unhinged, revealing the pressures of society, a yearn for the desire to escape a ‘disconnected’ world.

'60 Pulses' by Aliki Chiotaki (Greece) is a journey through the structures of ‘hierarchy, submitted to the entrapments of the fabrics of society . A woman makes her way through the concrete systems, the act of repetition attest the condition of the body as an elusive phenomenon that disappears even as it occurs. She falls only to rise again.

'Knock' by Running Man Australia is set in a dystopian world, where we see a man siting alone in a room, disconnected from the world, his business attire denotes ‘work’. In a parallel world, we are then taken to three young boys having a sleep over, telling each horror stories. A cross-over occurs between the man's life in disconnection and the future of one of the boy's very own reality. Questioning the route from childhood to adulthood, and whether the design is predefined.

A constant thread with all four dance films, is the fantasy to escape, reclaim, reconnect & return to our innate body.
